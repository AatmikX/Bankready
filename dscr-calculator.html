What DSCR means

The debt service coverage ratio tells a bank how many times your yearly cash flow covers your yearly loan repayments. A DSCR of 1.5x means you earn ₹1.50 for every ₹1 you owe that year.

How it is calculated

DSCR = (PAT + Depreciation + Term loan interest)
       ÷ (Term loan interest + Principal)

This calculator uses the equivalent shortcut: EBITDA less tax and working capital interest, divided by all term loan EMIs.

Typical bank thresholds

Average DSCR, most term loans1.50x+
Minimum in any single year1.20–1.25x
FOIR, salaried home loans50–60%

Norms vary by bank, sector and loan size. Treat these as a guide.
